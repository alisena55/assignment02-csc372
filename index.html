About UNCG Outdoor Adventures

Our program is designed to provide students with safe, educational, and exciting outdoor experiences. We handle the logistics and gear so you can focus on the adventure.

  • Access to high-quality rental equipment for personal trips.
  • Led by trained student guides certified in Wilderness First Aid.
  • Opportunities to develop leadership and outdoor survival skills.
