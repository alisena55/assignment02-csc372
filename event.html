Fall Break Waterfall Exploration

Category: Multi-Day Expedition

Date/Time: Oct 9, 8:00 AM – Oct 13, 5:00 PM

Location: Western NC

Spend your Fall Break exploring the most iconic hidden waterfalls across Western North Carolina on this guided multi-day expedition.

Trip Description

This expedition takes you deep into the Appalachian mountains to discover secluded waterfalls. We will set up a base camp and take daily hikes to different falls, ranging from easy walks to strenuous, steep climbs over wet terrain.

Attendee Expectations

  • Must be comfortable hiking 5 to 8 miles a day on uneven, slippery trails.
  • Highly breathable, quick-drying trail running shoes with aggressive outsoles are mandatory for slick rocks and creek crossings.
  • Attend the mandatory pre-trip gear check at the Rec Center.

Itinerary

  • Friday (10/9): Depart campus at 8:00 AM, arrive at the base camp, and do a short afternoon hike.
  • Saturday - Monday (10/10 - 10/12): Full days of hiking to multiple waterfall locations, with swimming opportunities depending on weather.
  • Tuesday (10/13): Break camp, hike one final trail, and return to Greensboro by 5:00 PM.

Accessibility & Participation Info

Due to the rugged terrain and slippery conditions around the waterfalls, this trip requires good balance and physical mobility. If you require accommodations or want to discuss alternative trips better suited to your needs, please contact the trip leaders in advance.

Event Details

  • Date: Oct 9 - 13, 2026
  • Departure Time: 8:00 AM
  • Location: Depart from Campus Rec Center
  • Organizer: UNCG Outdoor Adventures
  • Admission: $160.00 (includes transportation, camping fees, and group gear)
